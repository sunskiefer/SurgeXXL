SurgeXXL Q-LINK MAP

Number the controls in the order you want them on the knobs. 1-8 is the first Q-Link page (Force knobs 1-8), 9-16 the second, and so on. Tap a control on the picture to give it the next number (tap again to take it off), or type a number in the list. To leave a knob empty, type a higher number (skipping one leaves that knob empty) or use Leave empty under the knobs. Your numbers save automatically.
